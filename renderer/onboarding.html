Handrail

Handrail watches your screen and walks you through things

Open the app you're stuck in, tell Handrail what you're trying to do, and it guides you through it — pointing at the actual buttons, on your actual screen.

  • It can see what you see Every question includes a screenshot, so it answers about your screen — not in general.
  • It points at the thing An arrow appears on your screen at the control you need next.
  • It keeps up with you For longer tasks it watches for each step being done and moves on by itself.
Step 1 of 3

Connect an AI provider

Handrail needs a key to think with. Paste one below — it stays on this computer and is only ever sent to the provider it belongs to.

Works with OpenRouter, OpenAI and Anthropic keys — Handrail recognises which is which. Where do I get a key?

Everything stays on your machine. Screenshots, your key, and every conversation are stored locally. The only thing that leaves this computer is the request you send to the AI provider. There is no telemetry.

Step 2 of 3

Let Handrail see your screen

Handrail takes a screenshot when you ask it something, so it can answer about what's actually in front of you. Nothing is recorded and nothing is uploaded anywhere except to your AI provider.

Handrail will ask your operating system for permission the first time it takes a screenshot.
